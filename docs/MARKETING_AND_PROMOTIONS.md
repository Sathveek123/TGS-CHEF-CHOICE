# 📣 TGS ChefChoice — Regional Marketing & Promotions Strategy (`MARKETING_AND_PROMOTIONS.md`)

This document presents the strategic marketing plan, regional promotion ideas, and social media campaigns designed to grow **TGS ChefChoice** in the Kasibugga & Palasa region.

---

## 🎯 1. Target Audience & Regional Demographics

TGS ChefChoice serves three primary customer segments in the Palasa/Kasibugga municipal area:

1. **Local Family Dining:** Families seeking authentic, hygienic Andhra & North Indian curries, biryani, and festive spreads.
2. **Youth & College Students:** Groups gathering for wok-starters (**Chicken 555**, Chilli Chicken, Noodles), burgers, Belgian waffles, and thick shakes.
3. **Highway & Drive-Through Travelers:** Commuters along the NH16 corridor looking for quick takeaway or drive-through pickup.

---

## 💡 2. High-Impact Promotion Campaigns

### 🌾 Campaign A: Sankranti & Festival Thali Feast
- **Concept:** Traditional full-course Andhra Thali served during festival seasons (Sankranti, Ugadi, Dussehra).
- **Digital Angle:** Dedicated card in `offers.html` with advance WhatsApp booking prompt.
- **WhatsApp Copy:** *"Book your Sankranti Andhra Thali Feast a day ahead — limited 50 thalis daily!"*

### 🎂 Campaign B: Free Birthday Dessert
- **Concept:** Any customer booking a table for a birthday celebration gets a complimentary dessert plate on the house.
- **Digital Angle:** Option selector in `order.html` (`Occasion: Birthday Celebration`).
- **Call-to-Action:** Highlighted on home page and offers page.

### 🍱 Campaign C: Sunday Family Combo Spread
- **Concept:** Discounted pricing on **"The Family Spread"** package (₹280–340/person) for group reservations of 4+ guests on Sundays.
- **Goal:** Drive high-ticket weekend dining room volume.

---

## 📱 3. WhatsApp & Social Growth Strategy

Since WhatsApp is the primary digital communication channel in Kasibugga:

1. **WhatsApp Broadcast List:** Create a broadcast list of regular customers to announce weekly specials every Friday morning.
2. **Table QR Code Contest:** Stick QR codes on all tables encouraging customers to leave a Google Review in exchange for a 5% discount on their next visit.
3. **Instagram Food Reels:** Post 15-second high-heat wok videos of **Chicken 555** flaming in the wok, tagging local Palasa/Srikakulam food bloggers.
