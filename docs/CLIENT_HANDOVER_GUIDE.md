## 📱 1. How WhatsApp Ordering Works for You

When a customer uses your website ([tgs-chef-choice.vercel.app](https://tgs-chef-choice.vercel.app/)), they do **not** pay online. Instead, their order or table reservation opens directly on your phone's WhatsApp (`097013 25292`).

### What a Message Looks Like:
```
*New Order — TGS ChefChoice* 🛒

*Order ID:* TGS-260723-4821
*Name:* Shanmukh A
*Phone:* 9876543210
*Type:* Dine-In

*Items Ordered:*
• 1 × Hyd Chicken Dum Biryani — ₹200
• 1 × Chicken 555 — ₹260

*Total: ₹460*

_Sent via tgs-chef-choice.vercel.app_
```

### What You Should Do:
1. **Read the message:** Check the Reference ID, items ordered, and total amount.
2. **Reply to the customer:** Send a quick confirmation reply (see section 3 below).
3. **Pass to Kitchen:** Tell the kitchen team to prepare the items.
4. **Collect Payment:** Collect payment in person (cash, UPI, GPay, PhonePe) when the customer arrives or when delivery is made.

## 📌 2. Setting Up QR Code Table Ordering

You can let customers sit at a table, scan a QR code on their phone, view the menu, and order directly to your WhatsApp!

### Step-by-step Setup:
1. Open any free QR Code Generator (like `qr-code-generator.com`).
2. Create QR codes for each table using these exact links:
   - **Table 1:** `https://tgs-chef-choice.vercel.app/menu?table=1`
   - **Table 2:** `https://tgs-chef-choice.vercel.app/menu?table=2`
   - **Table 3:** `https://tgs-chef-choice.vercel.app/menu?table=3`
   - **Table 4:** `https://tgs-chef-choice.vercel.app/menu?table=4` ... etc.
3. Print and laminate the QR code stickers.
4. Stick one QR code on each physical table inside the restaurant.
5. When a customer scans Table 3's QR code, the website automatically shows **"📍 Ordering for Table 3"** at the top of their screen, and their WhatsApp message will say `*Table:* Table 3`!
